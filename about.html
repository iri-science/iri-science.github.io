Scientific discovery across infrastructure

The Department of Energy's Integrated Research Infrastructure (IRI) effort helps researchers combine experimental facilities, observational data, advanced computing, and scientific services in workflows that cross organizational boundaries. The scientific goal is to make these tools work together to accelerate discovery.

IRI builds on the computing and networking foundations of DOE's Advanced Scientific Computing Research (ASCR) facilities. Its implementation community now includes a wider range of research infrastructure, bringing new requirements and operational experience to the shared approach.

The Facility API provides common interfaces and reusable software within that wider effort. Engagement, trust, policy, and governance help make technical integration useful and sustainable. Participating providers retain control of their resources, access policies, allocations, and operations.

Explore organizations implementing IRI, documented practical results, and ways to participate.

Connecting IRI and the American Science Cloud

IRI's shared resource and operation interfaces support integration with the American Science Cloud (AmSC). Project context and identity integration can connect workflows with participating services while each provider retains its own authorization decisions. Read the documented AmSC integration story.

Science pathfinders

Science use cases help identify the data movement, computing, coordination, and trust requirements of cross-facility work. The pathfinder pages preserve examples of that scientific motivation.

  • Light sources: Advanced Light Source, Advanced Photon Source, Linac Coherent Light Source, and National Synchrotron Light Source II.
  • Plasma physics and fusion energy: DIII-D workflows linking experimental and computing resources.
  • Climate and Earth science: Earth System Grid Federation 2.
