The [National Research Platform](https://nrp.ai/about/) (NRP) is taking an important step toward integration with the American Science Cloud (AmSC). John Graham of San Diego Supercomputer Center has joined the Integrated Research Infrastructure (IRI) subcommittee and is implementing the IRI API on NRP infrastructure, helping extend the reach of shared scientific services.

Supported in part by the National Science Foundation, NRP pools computing and storage resources across more than 70 institutions. Its distributed Kubernetes cluster, Nautilus, supports research and education in areas including artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-intensive science. Participating institutions contribute resources to a shared platform, expanding what researchers can accomplish together.

ESnet’s Justas Balcas supported the implementation by combining the IRI reference toolkit with custom integrations addressing NRP’s specific needs. The collaboration demonstrates how reusable tools and shared interfaces can accommodate the practical requirements of different research infrastructures.

IRI aims to help researchers and scientific workflows use resources across facilities through interoperable interfaces. Bringing those interfaces to NRP creates a foundation for connecting its capabilities with AmSC workflows. Graham’s participation also brings NRP’s operational experience into the IRI community, helping inform the continued development of interfaces that serve a broader range of scientific infrastructure.
