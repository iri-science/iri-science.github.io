The IRI Interfaces Technical Subcommittee gathers requirements, defines use cases, and specifies unified, interoperable interfaces for DOE research facilities and infrastructure partners. Building on its work with DOE ASCR facilities, the subcommittee supports the American Science Cloud (AmSC) and the Genesis Mission by enabling the integration of computing, storage, data, networking, and scientific services across participating facilities and partner infrastructures.

Its responsibilities include identifying integration requirements across facilities and partners, coordinating interface specifications and implementation guidance, and addressing interoperability challenges. Beyond defining Application Programming Interfaces (APIs), this includes integration with front-end applications such as Jupyter notebooks and with orchestration systems, including hosted orchestration tools, to support scientific and AI workflows.

The subcommittee works to improve the user experience for discovering, accessing, and managing resources and complex workflows across high-performance computing, data, and scientific network facilities. It considers applicable standards, builds on other organizations' work, and develops reference IRI interface implementations and reusable integration patterns for AmSC and Genesis Mission participants, while respecting facility and partner policies, capabilities, and operational requirements.
