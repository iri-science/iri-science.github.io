IRI @ SC26

November 15–20, 2026 · McCormick Place, Chicago, Illinois

Meet the Integrated Research Infrastructure (IRI) team at Department of Energy booth #4829 during SC26. Two live demonstrations will show resource discovery with IRI 2.0 and scientific workflows spanning DOE facilities and ESnet's AWS environment.

Both demonstrations run on Monday, November 16, and Wednesday, November 18. The announcement includes the times, demonstration stations, presenters, and details of each workflow.

Demo details and schedule

IRI Developer & User Community Work Session

Tuesday, November 17, 11:00 a.m. CST

Join an open working session for Integrated Research Infrastructure developers, facility teams, and users to discuss current implementations, integration challenges, priorities, and future capabilities. Bring questions, use cases, and feedback to help shape the next steps for IRI.

Led by Paul Rich (ANL) and John MacAuley (ESNet).
