Integrated Research Infrastructure

Connecting research infrastructure. Enabling science across facilities.

IRI helps researchers combine computing, data, instruments, and scientific services across organizations. Shared interfaces and reusable software make it easier to discover capabilities and build workflows across independently operated infrastructure.

Building on its DOE computing and networking foundations, IRI is expanding with a wider research community. Providers retain control of their resources, access policies, and operations.
