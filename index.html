A shared language for infrastructure

IRI 2.0: Flexible, Discoverable, and Agentic

Development specification · Draft

The IRI Facility API 2.0 development specification gives software a richer way to understand research infrastructure: what resources are, how they connect, and which operations a provider advertises. Shared resource types, profiles, and machine-readable contracts support conventional workflow tools and authorized AI agents.
