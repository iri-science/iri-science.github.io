Program direction and implementation

DOE's Office of Science leads the organization and governance of the IRI effort. Federal management and oversight involve ASCR, the science programs, and the Office of the Deputy Director for Science Programs. The Management Council organizes technical and engagement efforts in the field.

Technical contributions through the Interfaces subcommittee and RFC process complement this program governance. Repository review provides a path to develop shared interfaces; it does not replace DOE program decisions.

Management Council Executive Committee

The Executive Committee provides institutional oversight and works with Office of Science leaders and program officers to align IRI objectives, facility missions, and resources. Its responsibilities include setting priorities, coordinating resources subject to DOE approval where required, reviewing progress, and addressing risks.

Technical subcommittees

Outreach and Engagement

Coordinates scientific use cases and requirements, connects pathfinder teams, and supports wider community participation. Outreach and Engagement membership and remit.

Interfaces

Gathers cross-facility requirements and develops interoperable interfaces, specifications, and implementation guidance for research facilities and infrastructure partners. This includes connections with researcher-facing tools and orchestration systems. Interfaces membership and remit.

Trusted IRI Designs (TRUSTID)

Develops trust and cybersecurity design patterns for performant cross-facility scientific workflows, in collaboration with facility and infrastructure security stakeholders. TRUSTID membership and remit.

Bring implementation experience and proposed technical changes through the RFC Process and the community contribution paths.

Formation history: FY2024–2025

In FY2024, the Office of Science directed the ASCR facilities to establish the Management Council. The FY2024–2025 agency priority goal focused on establishing IRI governance, a steering model, and membership to advance integrated science goals, building on the 2023 Architecture Blueprint Activity report.

Formation plans from this period anticipated broader participation and structured user forums for infrastructure providers and science teams.
